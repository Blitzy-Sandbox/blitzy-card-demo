Part Four

Quality & Risk

Eight validation gates gate the migration; known risks are tracked with explicit mitigations.

Assurance

Eight validation gates

  • G1End-to-end boundary
  • G2Zero-warning build
  • G3Performance baseline
  • G4Named real-world artifacts
  • G5Contract verification
  • G6Unsafe-code audit
  • G7Scope matching (Extended)
  • G8Integration sign-off

Gate 8 consolidates coverage (≥80% line), an OWASP dependency-check with zero critical/high CVEs, and 100% paragraph traceability.
